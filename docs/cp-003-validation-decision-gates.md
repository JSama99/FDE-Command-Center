# CP-003.1 — Validation Decision Gates

Assign a gate only after all five sessions are scored. No gate may be
assigned from fewer sessions, from discussion alone, or from
enthusiasm without observed behavior.

## GO

All of: median task total ≥ 7/10; zero unresolved critical-error
flags (every flag was corrected in debrief and traced to a fixable
cause); median usefulness ≥ 18/25; at least two participants took a
concrete next step (named workflow, introduction, or budget
discussion — not mere interest). Then: justify planning a more secure
pilot (CP-001 R-01–R-04 still required before any customer data).

## ITERATE

Value is evident (median usefulness ≥ 15/25 or a clear value pattern)
but: a repeated critical error, a task scoring ≤1/2 for ≥3
participants, or friction that blocks T4/T5. Then: fix the ranked
changes in the results template and re-test the affected tasks only.

## NO-GO

Any of: participants cannot distinguish hypothesis from root cause
after facilitation; median usefulness < 15/25 with no value pattern;
a critical evidence-integrity misunderstanding that the workflow
itself causes (not merely fails to prevent); no concrete next steps.
Then: stop validation; do not reframe as a marketing problem.

## Non-gates (never sufficient alone)

Verbal praise, willingness to "try it sometime", facilitator's own
confidence, or smooth demo operation.
